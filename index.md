Hi, I’m Malcolm, a master’s student in biophysics working on my thesis in complex fluid dynamics at the Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization.

My day-to-day work typically involves a good mix of coffee runs, numerical simulations and pen and paper calculations. 
Currently I'm keen on applying methods from statistical physics to biological systems. In the best case one can start from a microscopic picture and use mean-field theory and informed approximations to arrive at a thermodynamically consistent model that still captures the essential physics. This allows for a high interpretability of simulation results. 

Another direction that has become a major focus of mine is the study of non-equilibrium dynamics by expanding around equilibrium systems using linear response theory. In particular I would like to understand better how complex mixtures respond to changes in their environment and how one can leverage this to design systems with desired properties such as high stability or elevated sensetivity to the concentrations of selected components.
